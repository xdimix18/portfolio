01 — About

I started with hardware — microcontrollers, sensors and a soldering iron — and that path led me to servers and language models. Today my main focus is self-hosted AI: making models, databases and tooling run reliably on my own infrastructure, with real authentication, monitoring and backups — not just a demo notebook.

In practice that means a lot of Python (Flask, FastAPI), Docker Compose and Linux administration, networking and reverse proxies, plus occasional C for embedded work. I like to understand a system from the bottom up: from how a packet is routed to why a model started hallucinating.

I also worked at a computer repair shop, so the hardware side isn't foreign to me — diagnostics, component replacement, everything in between. That taught me to keep working on a problem until it actually works, not until it looks like it does.
